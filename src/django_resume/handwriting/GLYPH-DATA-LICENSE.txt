glyph_data.json
===============

glyph_data.json holds the glyph outlines, hand-traced writing strokes, advances
and ligatures used to render the editorial theme's self-writing labels. The
outlines are derived from the Astagina Signature font by Green Adventure STD
and are covered by the same terms as the font, not by the django-resume
license. See
django_resume/static/django_resume/fonts/script/ASTAGINA-LICENSE.txt.
